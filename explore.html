छठ पूजा

An ancient 4-day pilgrimage of sun worship, purity, and supreme devotion.

छठ पूजा क्या है?

What is Chhath Puja?

छठ पूजा सूर्य देव और छठी मैया की उपासना का चार दिनों तक चलने वाला एक महान हिन्दू महापर्व है। यह मुख्य रूप से बिहार, झारखंड, उत्तर प्रदेश और नेपाल के तराई क्षेत्रों में अपार श्रद्धा से मनाया जाता है। इस व्रत में उगते और डूबते सूर्य देव को अर्घ्य दिया जाता है, जो पूरी सृष्टि को ऊर्जा और जीवन प्रदान करते हैं।

Chhath Puja is an ancient 4-day Vedic festival dedicated to Surya Dev (the Sun God) and Chhathi Maiya. Celebrated primarily in Bihar, Jharkhand, Uttar Pradesh, and Nepal, it is the only Hindu festival where offerings are made to both the setting and rising sun, symbolizing the cycle of birth, life, and gratitude.

॥ महिमा और उद्देश्य ॥

लोग यह व्रत क्यों रखते हैं?

Why People Take Part in Chhath Puja

☀️

सूर्य देव के प्रति आभार

Gratitude to the Sun

Surya Dev is the visible deity (*Pratyaksha Devata*) who sustains all life. Devotees offer Arghya to thank Him for health, prosperity, and light.

🌊

कठोर अनुशासन व शुचिता

Purity & Self-Discipline

Observing a 36-hour waterless fast (*Nirjala Vrat*) without drinking a single drop of water, cooked over traditional clay stoves (*Mitti ka Chulha*).

🌾

संतान रक्षा व परिवार की खुशहाली

Protection & Family Prosperity

Chhathi Maiya (Shashthi Devi) is revered as the mother who protects newborns, blesses childless couples, and brings longevity to family members.

🪔

सामाजिक समानता

Social Equality & Unity

Chhath breaks all social and caste barriers. Everyone stands waist-deep in the same river waters, offering the same bamboo *soop* laden with *thekua*.

॥ चार दिन ॥

चार दिवसीय महापर्व

The Four Sacred Ritual Days

दिन 1

नहाय-खाय

Nahay Khay — Purity and Preparation

Devotees take a holy dip in sacred rivers, clean their homes, and prepare pure saattvik food (*Lauki Bhaat* with rock salt) cooked in brass/bronze utensils.

दिन 2

खरना

Kharna — The Evening Ritual

A full-day fast concluding in the evening. Devotees prepare jaggery kheer (*Rasiya*) and roti on clay stoves, offer it to Chhathi Maiya, and begin the 36-hour waterless fast.

दिन 3

संध्या अर्घ्य

Sandhya Arghya — Evening Offering

Devotees gather at riverbanks carrying bamboo *soop* filled with fresh *thekua*, sugarcane, and fruits. Standing waist-deep in water, Arghya is offered to the setting sun.

दिन 4

उषा अर्घ्य

Usha Arghya — Morning Offering

Before dawn, devotees return to the riverbank. Milk and water are offered to the rising sun (*Usha*), marking the successful completion of the festival and breaking of the fast (*Parana*).

छठ पूजा का इतिहास व कथाएँ

Ancient Legends & History

वैदिक काल

ऋग्वेद और सूर्य पूजा

Surya Dev worship originates from the oldest Vedic texts. Rishis used to channel solar energy directly by fasting and meditating under the morning sun rays.

रामायण काल

श्री राम व माता सीता

According to legend, upon returning to Ayodhya after defeating Ravana, Lord Rama and Mata Sita performed Surya worship and kept Chhath fast in the Kartik month.

महाभारत काल

दानवीर कर्ण और द्रौपदी

Karna (son of Surya) offered Arghya standing waist-deep in water daily. Draupadi & the Pandavas also performed Chhath Puja to reclaim their lost kingdom.
